Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Overview

At VidSavor (accessible at vidsavor.com), the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance. This document describes the types of information handled by our system and our commitment to client-side confidentiality.

2. No Personal Credentials Required

VidSavor does not require user registration, email sign-ups, passwords, or personal identity details. You can freely process public online media links without having an account.

3. Transient Link Processing

When you submit a URL for media extraction, our service processes that link transiently to locate publicly accessible CDN video and audio streams. We do not store, catalog, or archive user-submitted URLs, IP addresses, or downloaded media files on our storage servers.

4. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any modern website, VidSavor may use essential cookies to maintain browser session preferences. We do not employ intrusive tracking cookies or cross-site tracking scripts.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, please feel free to contact our administrative team via email at support@vidsavor.com or via our Contact Us page.
