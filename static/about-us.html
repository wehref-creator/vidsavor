About VidSavor

VidSavor was built with a single goal: to provide the fastest, cleanest, and most reliable web-based media extractor without intrusive popups, forced software installs, or login barriers.

Our Technology Philosophy

We believe that online media archiving should be private, instantaneous, and high-fidelity. By optimizing stream parsing algorithms, VidSavor allows users to preserve public videos in their native original 1080p and 4K bitrates, as well as isolate audio tracks into high quality 320kbps MP3s.

Supported Platforms

VidSavor supports extracting public media from Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Twitter / X, Pinterest, and hundreds of standard media platforms.
