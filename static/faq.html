Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using VidSavor safely, legally, and easily.

How does auto-fetch on paste work?

When you click the "Paste" button or paste via keyboard (Ctrl+V) into the URL field, VidSavor detects the link and begins fetching available video, photo, and audio streams automatically without requiring any extra click.

Is VidSavor 100% free forever?

Yes. There are no subscriptions, hidden payments, or limits. You can download as many videos, reels, and audio tracks as you need.

Do downloaded videos have watermarks?

No. VidSavor delivers raw, original stream files directly from content distribution networks without adding logos, text, or watermarks.

Can I download from private accounts?

To respect user privacy and security protocols, VidSavor only downloads media from public accounts and public posts.

Does VidSavor store my downloads on its servers?

No. We maintain a zero-log, privacy-first system. All media is fetched transiently directly to your device storage without any file retention on our servers.

Can I convert videos to MP3 audio?

Yes. When you paste a video from YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram, our system extracts the highest bitrate audio stream and gives you a one-click MP3 download option.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes, VidSavor is a progressive web app (PWA) fully optimized for iOS Safari, Android Chrome, macOS, Windows, and Linux.

Who should I contact if a link fails?

If you encounter an error with a public link, please verify the post is still live and public, or reach out to our team via our Contact Us page.
