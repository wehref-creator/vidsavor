How to Download on Different Devices

iPhone & iPad (iOS Safari)

  1. Open the Instagram app and tap the Share icon (paper airplane).
  2. Select Copy Link to save the post URL to your clipboard.
  3. Open Safari, go to VidSavor, and tap Paste.
  4. Tap Download and confirm Safari's download prompt.
  5. Open Safari's Downloads list, tap the video, and tap Save Video to move it into your iPhone Camera Roll.

Android (Google Chrome / Edge)

  1. In Instagram, tap the three dots or Share icon and tap Copy Link.
  2. Open Google Chrome or your default browser, visit VidSavor, and tap Paste.
  3. Tap Download. Chrome will automatically save the clean MP4 file to your Downloads folder and native Gallery.

Windows & Mac Computers

  1. Navigate to the Instagram post in your desktop browser and copy the address bar URL.
  2. Paste the link into VidSavor's input bar and click Download.
  3. Choose your preferred resolution (1080p, 720p, or MP3) to download directly to your PC or Mac.
