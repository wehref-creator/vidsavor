Notice and Procedure for Making Claims of Copyright Infringement

1. Adherence to 17 U.S.C. § 512

VidSavor respects the intellectual property rights of creators and strictly adheres to the provisions of Title 17, United States Code, Section 512 (DMCA). It is our policy to respond promptly to clear, formal notices of alleged copyright infringement.

2. No Media Hosting

VidSavor does not store, re-host, broadcast, or duplicate copyrighted video or audio streams on our server infrastructure. All extraction operations function client-side or as transient pointers to publicly exposed endpoints on third-party host servers.

3. Filing a Takedown Notice

If you are an authorized copyright holder or legal representative and wish to request link restrictions or blacklist specific URL patterns, please send a written notice containing:

  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  • Specific URLs on our service or original platform URLs to be blacklisted.
  • Contact information including your legal name, physical address, and email.
  • A statement of good faith belief and accuracy under penalty of perjury.
