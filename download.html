Installation & Setup Instructions

Select your platform below for a simple, 4-step setup guide.

1

Download ZIP Archive

Click Download Extension (.zip) and extract the folder to a convenient location on your PC or Mac.

2

Open Extensions Page

Navigate to chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions in Microsoft Edge) in your browser address bar.

3

Enable Developer Mode

Turn ON the toggle switch labeled Developer mode in the top-right corner of the extensions page.

4

Load Unpacked

Click "Load unpacked" and select the unzipped extension directory. Pin VidSavor to your toolbar for instant 1-click access!

1

Download VidSavor APK

Tap Download Android APK to download the official v1.0.0 package directly to your phone.

2

Allow Installation

Open the downloaded APK and tap "Install". If prompted by Android, toggle "Allow from this source" in your settings.

3

Browse Any Reel or Video

Open Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, or Facebook. Find the video, reel, or audio clip you want to save.

4

Tap Share ➔ VidSavor

Tap the Share button and choose VidSavor from your system share options. The video is saved directly to your Gallery!

1

Add VidSavor Shortcut

On your iPhone or iPad, tap the "Add to Apple Shortcuts" button. The native iOS Shortcuts application will open automatically.

2

Confirm Permissions

Tap "Add Shortcut". When prompted for Photos access, tap "Allow" so VidSavor can save downloaded MP4s into your Camera Roll.

3

Tap Share on Instagram / TikTok

While watching any Reel, Short, or TikTok clip, tap the Share icon (the paper plane or arrow) to open the iOS Share Sheet.

4

Select VidSavor Downloader

Scroll down the action list and tap "VidSavor Downloader". Your video is extracted in original HD and appears in your Apple Photos app!

1

Open VidSavor in Browser

Visit vidsavor.com using Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, or Safari on your Windows, Mac, or Linux computer.

2

Click Install Icon

Look in the right side of your address bar for the install icon (a small computer screen or + sign), or tap browser menu ➔ "Install VidSavor".

3

Confirm Installation

Click "Install" in the confirmation popup. VidSavor will immediately launch in its own standalone window with no browser tabs.

4

Pin to Taskbar or Dock

Right-click the VidSavor icon in your Windows Taskbar or macOS Dock and select "Pin" for instant desktop access anytime!
