How to Use Meme Video Clips to Boost Social Media Engagement

Short cutaway reaction clips (often called vlips or meme b-roll) are standard weapons in every top YouTuber and Reel creator's arsenal. Inserting a 2-to-3 second reaction clip breaks visual monotony, triggers dopamine release, and increases average percentage viewed (APV) on YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

How to Overlay Meme Clips in Video Editors:

  • CapCut & VN Editor: Tap Overlay / PIP, import the downloaded MP4 meme clip, position it on the screen, and use Chroma Key to remove the green background if using a green screen clip.
  • Adobe Premiere Pro: Place the meme clip on video track V2 above your main timeline, apply Ultra Key for green screens, and synchronize the audio track.
