# VidSavor - Browser Web Extension (Manifest V3)

Download online videos, reels, shorts, stories, and audio in HD from **Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Twitter (X), and Pinterest** directly while browsing.

---

## 🚀 Features

- **🎯 Instant Tab Detection**: Automatically detects when you are watching a video or reel and provides a 1-click Download button in the extension popup.
- **✨ Injected Floating Action Button**: Seamless floating `⬇ Download HD` pill directly overlaid on Instagram Reels, TikTok videos, and YouTube Shorts.
- **🖱️ Right-Click Context Menu**: Right-click on any video, link, or media thumbnail and choose **"Download Video with VidSavor"** or **"Extract MP3 Audio with VidSavor"**.
- **📋 Clipboard Integration**: 1-click paste to download any social media URL directly.

---

## 📦 How to Install (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Opera)

1. Open your browser and navigate to the extension management page:
   - **Google Chrome**: Go to `chrome://extensions`
   - **Microsoft Edge**: Go to `edge://extensions`
   - **Brave**: Go to `brave://extensions`
   - **Opera**: Go to `opera://extensions`
2. Turn **ON** the **Developer mode** toggle (usually located in the top-right corner).
3. Click the **"Load unpacked"** button (or "Load unpacked extension").
4. Select this directory:
   ```
   C:\Users\B1\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\vidsavor\extension
   ```
5. The **VidSavor** icon will appear in your browser toolbar! Click the puzzle icon to **Pin** VidSavor for instant 1-click access.

## 🎬 How to Use

### Method 1: Popup 1-Click
1. Browse to any Instagram Reel, TikTok video, YouTube Short, Facebook post, or Twitter video.
2. Click the **VidSavor** icon in your browser toolbar.
3. Tap **"Download Now"** to instantly open your video streams and resolutions.

### Method 2: Floating Button on Video
1. When viewing any Reel or Short, look for the subtle purple **"Download HD"** pill at the bottom-right.
2. Click it to immediately prepare the video for download.

### Method 3: Right-Click Context Menu
1. Right-click on any video, thumbnail, or link.
2. Click **"Download Video with VidSavor"**.
